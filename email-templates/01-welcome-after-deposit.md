# Welcome email — sent after $250 deposit

**When to send:** Within 1 business day of Stripe deposit notification. Personalize the bracketed bits, then send.

**From:** jamie@gingeralchemy.com
**Subject:** You're in. Here's what happens next.
**Preview line:** Your $250 deposit is in. Here's the next 24–48 hours.

---

Hi [first name],

Your $250 deposit just hit my inbox. You're officially in the queue.

Here's what's about to happen.

Within the next 24–48 hours, three things will land in your inbox:

**Your district research brief** — demographics, cost of living, opponent voting record (if you have one), donor and PAC breakdowns, the local issues that should anchor your campaign. All sourced.

**Your race-specific Q&A** — interview questions built from your research. Not generic "tell us about yourself" stuff. Questions designed to surface the things that will make voters in your district decide to back you.

**A "gather this" checklist** — a short list of what you'll need before your build week: domain, photos, a few logistical bits.

You don't need to do anything until those three things land. You'll have time to read, react, and start prepping.

---

**If you want a head start, here are three things you can begin on your own time:**

A domain. The pattern most candidates use is `[yourname]for[state].com` — but go with whatever sounds like you. Any reliable registrar works (Namecheap, GoDaddy, ~$12/year). You'll own it from day one.

Photos. A few candid shots of you in your community. Real moments — knocking doors, at local events, with family if you're comfortable. Not professional headshots. Voters connect with people, not consultants.

A quiet 30–45 minutes for voice memos. When the Q&A lands, the easiest way to answer is to talk into your phone. Ramble, speak from the heart, no editing required. Your words become your website copy.

---

**What happens after you send your answers back:**

Once you've gone through the Q&A and gathered your materials (most candidates take a week or two — there's no clock from my end), you'll book a build week on my calendar and send the $500 build-week fee. That week, your site gets built. Unlimited revisions over the following 3 business days. Launch + final $250.

---

I'm rooting for you. The work you're doing matters more than most of us know how to say.

Talk soon,
Jamie

jamie@gingeralchemy.com

P.S. If anything shifts on your end — you decide not to run, your timeline changes, you have questions — just email me. This is built around real life, not a fixed funnel.
